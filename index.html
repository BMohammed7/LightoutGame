How to Play

  • The board is a 5×5 grid of lights (ON or OFF).
  • Clicking any tile flips that tile and its up, down, left, and right neighbours.
  • Turn all lights OFF to win.
  • Use Hint to highlight the next optimal tile without changing the board or move count.
  • Use Reset to restore the current puzzle, or New Game for a fresh solvable board.
